LEGAL GUIDANCE

Our Expert Answers

Get clear, reliable guidance from our experts to help you understand your legal questions with confidence.

To resolve land disputes, we first examine survey records, mutation certificates, and registered deeds. If title conflict exists, we file a Title Suit, partition suit, or seek injunctions in the appropriate District Court or High Court Division.
Under Section 498 of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), anticipatory bail is moved directly before the High Court Division. For regular bail, an application is submitted before the Sessions Judge and subsequently to the High Court Division if necessary.
A Writ Petition can be filed in the High Court Division when fundamental rights are violated, when public authorities act without lawful authority, or when statutory authorities fail to execute mandatory public duties.
We provide end-to-end corporate solutions including company incorporation, drafting Articles of Association (AoA), shareholder agreements, vendor contracts, NDA execution, and statutory regulatory compliance.
